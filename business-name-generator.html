What makes a great business name?

1. Easy to say & spell

If someone can't spell it from hearing it once, they can't Google it. Short wins.

2. A clean .com

A matching .com domain makes you look established and easy to remember.

3. Room to grow

Avoid names tied to one product — "Accra Cakes" can't sell furniture later.
